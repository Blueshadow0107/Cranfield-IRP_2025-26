Hints & clues: concrete technical moves

Channel / signal framing

  • Treat wavefront arrival as first-hitting time (FHT). Your probe time series is a molecular receiver; report mean arrival and its std under parameter uncertainty.
  • Peak = symbol amplitude. Use the PCE results: f dominates peak (S1 ≈ 0.65), eps dominates speed (S1 ≈ 0.59). This is your “channel parameter” story.
  • Non-coherent detection. You do not decode a phase; you decode whether a pulse arrived above a threshold within a window. Aligns with Lin et al. 2023.
  • Refractory = ISI. The 3.10 t.u. absolute refractory and 0.282/t.u. max 1:1 rate are the channel’s inter-symbol-interference budget.

UQ / PIML / reduced-order moves

  • Keep the Sobol/PCE result front and centre. It is the most Guo-aligned quantitative result you have and it is already computed.
  • Build a cheap surrogate. From the 384 PCE samples, fit a Gaussian process from (eps, f, phi, Du) → (speed, peak). Use it for fast inverse calibration to literature values.
  • Inverse recovery of eps/f/q. Given measured wave speed and peak, solve the inverse problem via the surrogate or closed-loop output error (Perrusquía–Guo style).
  • DMD/Koopman of snapshots. Run one spiral or target-wave case, save u/v snapshots, and compute DMD modes. Even one figure shows you can reduce the PDE to a modal picture.

Geometry / training moves

  • Demote anisotropy. It is realizable numerically but hard to control experimentally. Move it to “solver validation / future work”.
  • Pick one non-trivial training task. Band-pass/demux did not improve. Try a chemical diode (asymmetric transmission) or a pulse-routing T-junction with a genuinely non-uniform phi landscape.
  • Use CMA-ES, not DE, for small budgets. CMA adapts covariance; with ~200 evals it is your best shot at finding structure. But if it returns flat again, report it as evidence of weak trainability.
  • Keep walls + phi as separate controls. Walls give geometry; phi gives a continuous, experimentally relevant (photosensitive BZ) control field.

What to avoid

  • Do not claim you built a full chemical processor. You characterised components and attempted training.
  • Do not over-cite the Nature Cronin paper as if you reproduced it. Cite it as a benchmark and contrast your parameter-sensitivity approach.
  • Do not introduce 80-reaction FKN. Oregonator is enough for the thesis scope.
  • Do not drift into drone RL / 6G AI-RAN. Keep those as Guo’s other clusters only.
The PCE file already tells you the dominant knobs: eps and Du for speed; f and eps for peak. Build the thesis narrative around “who controls what” rather than around an untrained end-to-end gate.

  1. Scope: Is a characterisation-first thesis with a negative training result acceptable, or do you expect a demonstrably trained BZ circuit?

  6. ML expectation: How much learned modelling is enough to satisfy the “physics-informed ML” angle — a GP surrogate, DMD modes, or a full inverse problem?
  7. Viva defence: What is the weakest claim in the current draft that a reviewer/viva examiner is likely to attack?
  8. Training task: If I run one more 1–2 day optimisation, which task would you bet on: chemical diode, asymmetric T-junction, or pulse synchronisation detector?

Anti-patterns

Do not pivot to drone RL / 6G AI-RAN. Guo works there, but it is not your thesis. Mention only as his wider research context.
Do not start a wet-lab experiment in week 2. 10 days is numerics-only. Label any experimental claims as future work.
Do not drown in the FKN 80-reaction mechanism. Oregonator is validated and sufficient; cite FKN/Field–Noyes for chemistry grounding only.
Do not cite the wrong Weisi Guo. The organic-synthesis Guo is a different person; all papers above list Cranfield / Warwick / Turing-affiliated Weisi Guo.
Do not claim you reproduced the Nature Cronin chemical computer. Cite it as a benchmark and explain how your sensitivity/characterisation angle differs.
Do not hide the negative training result. A clean negative result (“uniform phi is already near-optimal for this task”) is publishable if framed honestly.
Do not overuse the “information dissipation” analogy. Numerical dissipation is not Kolmogorov turbulence; label the link as analogy/insight, not equivalence.
